The job reports.run checks every 15 minutes. When a slot has passed it stores one CSV snapshot in the platform database (last 7 per schedule) and writes the audit log. Nothing is emailed or sent anywhere: delivery needs a mail or storage setup that needs approval. A missed slot runs once. Snapshots are read with the same permissions as the report, checked again when you open them.
